CRM Implementation: How We Work

Business Challenges Addressed

SMEs often struggle with scattered lead info in emails, forgotten customer follow-ups, unorganized quotes, and zero visibility into team pipeline performance.

PBS Solution

We audit your customer lifecycle and configure a clean CRM structure (HubSpot, Zoho, or Salesforce) with custom pipeline stages, automated reminders, and instant lead capture forms.

Expected Outcomes

  • 100% lead capture visibility across all marketing channels.
  • Up to 35% improvement in sales conversion speed.
  • Real-time revenue reporting for company directors.

Implementation Process

  1. Step 1: Discovery & Mapping - Documenting sales stages.
  2. Step 2: Architecture & Setup - Configuring custom properties.
  3. Step 3: Migration - Scrubbing and importing existing data.
  4. Step 4: Testing & Training - Onboarding office & field staff.
  5. Step 5: Go-Live & Support - Active monitoring during rollout.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Services FAQ

How do you determine which CRM or software platform is best for us?
We evaluate your existing team size, process complexity, budget, and integration needs during our discovery audit. We remain platform-agnostic to recommend what actually serves your operational goals best.
Will our non-technical staff be able to adapt to the new system easily?
Yes! User adoption is a core pillar of our work. We design simplified interfaces, provide step-by-step role-based documentation, and conduct live interactive training workshops so your staff feels confident.
What does your Ongoing Support (AMC) cover?
Our AMC covers proactive monitoring of integrations, fixing workflow breaks, adding new pipeline stages or custom fields as your business grows, and priority helpdesk support for your staff.
