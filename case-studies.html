EDUCATION & ADMINISTRATION

Divine Fellowship School

Digital Administrative & Admission System Implementation

The Challenge

The institution relied heavily on manual paperwork, paper forms, and disjointed records for student admissions. Staff struggled with lost inquiries, unorganized guardian records, and intense administrative overhead during enrollment seasons.

Requirements

  • Digital student admission and onboarding workflow
  • Centralized database for student and guardian records
  • Automated email notifications for status updates
  • Clean staff workflow to eliminate paper tracking

The Delivered Solution

PBS audited the school's paper process and engineered an integrated administrative CRM system. The solution digitized student registration, created a role-based administrative portal for staff, and implemented automated notifications for inquiry updates.

Technologies Used

Custom Administrative Database • Automated Notification Engine • Role-Based Staff Dashboard • Google Workspace Integration

Verified Outcomes

Paperless Admissions

Fully digitized applicant logging and record capture.

Centralized Records

Single source of truth for student & guardian files.

Automated Follow-ups

Instant email confirmation for parents and staff.
